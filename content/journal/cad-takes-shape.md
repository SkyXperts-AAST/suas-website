Full UAV assembly is coming together in SolidWorks — propulsion, payload bay, landing gear, avionics, battery, and release mechanisms all integrated into one compact, serviceable layout.

Mid-design we made two big hardware calls: swapped to a new battery pack, and upgraded the companion computer from a Raspberry Pi 5 to a Jetson for more onboard AI headroom. Both meant redesigning the battery bay and electronics compartment from scratch.

Manufacturing drawings are next on the list.
