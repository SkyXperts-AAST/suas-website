Kicked off mechanical development by nailing down mission requirements: payload capacity, portability, endurance, structural strength, maintainability. After comparing configurations, we landed on a shifted-X quadcopter layout for better vision-system field of view without messing up load balance.

For payload delivery we looked at a few release concepts before settling on a servo-actuated flare release plus a parachute drop — both faster and safer than the alternatives we tried on paper.

We also spent time researching carbon fiber sandwich construction and cylindrical arm aerodynamics — groundwork for the CAD phase starting next.
