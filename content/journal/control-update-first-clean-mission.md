With the new gains locked in, we needed to know whether the fix held up in the air and not just on the bench. The test was deliberately small: a 3-waypoint autonomous mission with an automatic return-to-launch.

It flew clean. Three waypoints, RTL, landing, no drama. Not much of a mission, but it was the first time we could call the oscillation fix flight-proven rather than a good-looking number on a vibration plot, and we tested a lot more aggressively after it.
