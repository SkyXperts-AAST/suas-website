Repeated flight tests confirmed it: structural integrity and reliability hold under real operating conditions. The frame took flight and landing loads with zero damage, and our FEA predictions matched what we saw in the field.

Telemetry showed low vibration and stable flight throughout. Both payload mechanisms performed consistently.

This closes out the mechanical development cycle — concept, design, validation, manufacturing, testing, iteration — and leaves us with a platform we're confident bringing to competition.
