First real flight campaign. Ground checks (structural, vibration, propulsion, payload deployment) all passed — vibration logs from the flight controller looked clean, and both release mechanisms fired reliably.

Initial flights confirmed the structure held up, but flagged a couple of vibration sources and a CG tweak we wanted to make. We reinforced the arm mounts, rebalanced propulsion, and shuffled a few internal components around in response.

Noticeably smoother after the changes — final validation round is next.
