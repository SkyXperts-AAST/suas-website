Carbon fiber plates went through vacuum bagging, then got CNC'd to final shape. We built a full-scale prototype first just to sanity-check dimensions and part placement before committing to CF.

Arms and landing gear are 15mm carbon fiber tube, 9 layers multidirectional, motor mounts are 4mm laser-cut aluminum, with 3D-printed brackets tying the arms to the frame.

End result: our first complete flight-ready prototype.
