Before cutting anything, we ran FEA on the frame — thrust loads at the four motor mounts, weight and payload load at the center plate. The carbon fiber sandwich frame held up well within acceptable deformation.

We ran the numbers by hand too, to cross-check the sim and estimate natural frequency, plus some quick aero calcs on drag from the cylindrical arms in forward flight. Good balance of strength, weight, and efficiency — cleared to move to fabrication.
